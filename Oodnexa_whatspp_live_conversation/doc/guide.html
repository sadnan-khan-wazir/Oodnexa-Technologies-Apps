1. Architecture & Prerequisites

Oodnexa WhatsApp Live Conversation connects Odoo 19 directly to the official Meta WhatsApp Cloud API (Graph API v21.0). Unlike legacy solutions that rely on unofficial web scraping or third-party paid gateways (Twilio, MessageBird, Chat-API), this module uses Meta's official cloud endpoints.

Odoo Instance Odoo 19.0 Community or Enterprise Host ERP running Oodnexa WhatsApp module
Domain with SSL/HTTPS Valid HTTPS URL (e.g. https://odoo.mycompany.com) Required by Meta for Webhook real-time delivery
Meta Developer Account Free registration at developers.facebook.com Creates the WhatsApp Business App
Meta Business Portfolio Verified Business Manager account Manages WABA, Phone numbers, and System Users
Dedicated Phone Number Clean phone number not registered on regular WhatsApp app Official WhatsApp Business sending identity
Cost Advantage: Meta provides 1,000 free service conversations per month for every WhatsApp Business Account. You pay zero middleman subscription fees to third-party providers.

2. Phase 1: Meta Developer & WhatsApp Cloud API Setup

Follow these exact steps in the Meta Developer Console:

1 Create a Meta Developer Account

1. Navigate to https://developers.facebook.com/ and click Log In using your Facebook credentials.

2. If you are new, accept the developer terms to activate your developer profile.

2 Create a Meta Business App

1. In the Meta Developer Dashboard, click the green Create App button.

2. Choose Other → Click Next.

3. Select the App Type as Business (this provides access to WhatsApp Business API).

4. Enter your App Name (e.g., Oodnexa ERP WhatsApp) and select your Meta Business Portfolio / Account.

5. Click Create App.

3 Add the WhatsApp Product

1. On the App Dashboard product selection screen, locate WhatsApp.

2. Click Set Up.

3. Meta will initialize a sandbox environment with a test WhatsApp phone number and test recipient phone number for immediate testing.

4 Add Your Real WhatsApp Business Number

1. Under WhatsApp → API Setup, scroll down to Step 5: Add a phone number.

2. Click Add Phone Number.

3. Provide your WhatsApp Business Profile Display Name, Category, and Business Description.

4. Enter your dedicated phone number & choose verification method: SMS or Voice Call.

5. Enter the 6-digit verification code. Your number is now registered to Meta Cloud API!

Important: If the phone number is currently active on the WhatsApp consumer or WhatsApp Business mobile app on an iPhone or Android phone, you must delete that account from the WhatsApp app first before registering it to the Meta Cloud API.

3. Phase 2: Generating Permanent System User Token

The temporary access token generated in the API Setup tab expires in 24 hours. For production Odoo connectivity, you must create a Permanent System User Access Token that never expires.

1 Open Meta Business Manager Settings

1. Navigate to Meta Business Settings at https://business.facebook.com/settings.

2. Select the Business Account associated with your WhatsApp App.

2 Create a System User

1. In the left navigation menu, go to Users → System Users.

2. Click Add.

3. Set the System User Name (e.g., oodnexa_odoo_integration).

4. Set the Role to Admin.

5. Click Create System User.

3 Assign WhatsApp Assets to the System User

1. Select the new System User and click Add Assets.

2. Under Apps, select your WhatsApp Meta App and grant Full Control (Manage App).

3. Under WhatsApp Accounts, select your WhatsApp Business Account and grant Full Control (Manage WhatsApp Account).

4. Click Save Changes.

4 Generate Permanent Token with Required Permissions

1. Click Generate New Token next to the System User.

2. Select your WhatsApp App.

3. Set Token Expiration to Never.

4. Under Permissions, select the following two critical permissions:

  • whatsapp_business_management (Read & manage templates, account info)
  • whatsapp_business_messaging (Send & receive messages, media, voice notes)

5. Click Generate Token. Copy the token immediately and store it securely.

Security Best Practice: Treat this permanent token like an API password. Odoo encrypts and stores it securely in the database.

5. Phase 4: Webhook Configuration for 2-Way Live Chat

Webhooks enable real-time two-way messaging: when a customer sends a WhatsApp text, audio voice note, or photo, Meta immediately notifies Odoo via webhook.

1 Copy Webhook Details from Odoo

On your Odoo WhatsApp Provider record, locate the Webhook Configuration section:

  • Callback URL: https://your-odoo-domain.com/whatsapp/webhook/[token]
  • Verify Token: A secure unique random string generated by Odoo.

2 Configure Webhook in Meta Developer Console

1. In the Meta Developer Portal, go to WhatsApp → Configuration.

2. Locate the Webhook section and click Edit.

3. Paste the Callback URL copied from Odoo.

4. Paste the Verify Token copied from Odoo.

5. Click Verify and Save. Meta will send a verification ping to Odoo. Once validated, a green checkmark will appear.

3 Subscribe to Webhook Event Fields

1. Under the Webhook section in Meta, click Manage Webhook fields.

2. Find messages and click Subscribe.

3. (Optional) Subscribe to message_template_status_update for instant template approvals.

4. Click Done.

SSL Requirement: Meta will refuse Webhook URLs that use HTTP or self-signed SSL certificates. Your Odoo instance must have a trusted SSL certificate (e.g., Let's Encrypt, Cloudflare, or standard CA certificate).

6. Phase 5: Using WhatsApp Live Chat & 1-Click Quotations

With your Meta integration and Webhook configured, your team can begin managing customer conversations inside Odoo.

1 Launching Split-Screen Live Console

1. In the Odoo main navigation, go to WhatsApp → Messages → Live Conversations.

2. The console opens in a full split-screen layout:

  • Left Pane (Conversation Drawer): Displays all active customer chats, customer name, avatar initials, phone number, timestamp of latest message, and green unread count pills.
  • Center Pane (Message Thread): WhatsApp message history, incoming/outgoing bubbles, status indicators (Sent, Delivered, Read), voice note player, and input bar.
  • Right Pane (Customer Profile): Linked Odoo Partner card, contact details, and quick action buttons.

2 1-Click Quotation Creation from Active Chat

During an active conversation with a potential buyer:

  1. Click the green "CREATE QUOTATION" button on the top-right action bar.
  2. Odoo automatically opens the Sales Quotation modal with the customer already pre-filled.
  3. Add product order lines, quantities, and pricing.
  4. Click "Confirm & Send WhatsApp Quotation".
  5. Odoo immediately generates the official Quotation PDF, confirms the order, and dispatches the quote summary with downloadable link/PDF back to the customer's WhatsApp chat thread!

3 Inbox Filter Tabs & Real-Time Search

Quickly organize customer chats using the top filter pills:

  • All: Shows all active customer discussions.
  • Unread: Filters down to customers awaiting an agent response.
  • My Messages: Shows conversations specifically assigned to the logged-in agent.
  • Search Bar: Real-time filtering by customer name, phone number, or message text.

7. Phase 6: Voice Notes, Media & Mobile Responsive Usage

1 Recording & Sending Live Voice Notes

Give customers a personal touch by sending authentic voice messages:

  1. In the bottom reply bar, click the Microphone icon.
  2. Allow microphone access in your browser when prompted.
  3. A recording timer will begin counting (e.g. 00:15).
  4. Click the Send button (green check) to immediately encode and send the audio note, or click the red Cancel button to discard.
  5. The voice note appears as a native WhatsApp audio player with a play/pause button, scrubber, and waveform!

2 Transmitting Videos, Images & Documents

Click the Paperclip (Attach) icon to send rich media:

  • Images (JPG, PNG, WEBP): Transmitted with interactive lightbox viewer.
  • Videos (MP4, 3GP): Plays natively inside WhatsApp on mobile.
  • PDF Documents: Send brochures, technical specification sheets, and contracts.

3 Mobile & Tablet Responsive Operations

Oodnexa WhatsApp Live Conversation is engineered with a mobile-first philosophy:

  • On mobile smartphones (< 992px), the console intelligently presents a single-pane interface.
  • Tap any customer to slide into the chat thread.
  • Tap the back arrow ← to return to the conversation list.
  • The mobile voice recorder and camera upload buttons are thumb-friendly for fast field operations.

8. Phase 7: Automations, Troubleshooting & Meta FAQ

The 24-Hour Customer Service Window (Crucial Meta Policy)

Meta enforces a 24-hour customer service window:

  • Within 24 Hours of Customer's Last Message: You can send any free-form text, voice note, quotation, image, or video with zero restrictions.
  • After 24 Hours: To re-engage a customer who has been inactive for over 24 hours, you must use an Approved WhatsApp Message Template (HSM). Once the customer replies, a new 24-hour free-form session begins.
